5Wardro on the web — laptop and desktop

Sidebar, a wide working area and a right-hand rail, full-bleed. The rail does double duty: pick a piece in the grid and it becomes that piece's detail (last worn, wear count, outfits it belongs to, remove); with nothing selected it shows wardrobe stats and the untouched prompt. Everything here works: sidebar nav and category list, live search, the three filters, wear logging, remove, Edit details (rename, recategorise, retag in the rail), the outfit builder (click a slot, pick from the rail), favourite and delete on outfits, editable account fields, renameable categories, reminder toggles, a staged export, and Add photos — click the dropzone, sort each photo into a category, save. Reset this session in Settings puts it all back.

4Outfits — swipe deck and manual builder

Both are live. On 4a tap the left or right half of the collage to move through saved outfits; the + opens the builder. Wear today logs it for the date and offers to photograph the outfit on you; the photo joins that outfit's history, shown as thumbnails on the card. On 4b tap a slot to open the picker, choose a piece, tag occasion and season — Save stays inactive until a top, bottom and shoes are set. The photo of you in it is optional: take one and it becomes the saved outfit's card, skip it and the card shows the four pieces instead. Saved outfits appear first in the deck. Outfits now live inside the main prototype too — the Outfits tab in 2a opens the real card deck, browse list and builder, and Sign-up above the phone starts at create-account so the Male / Female choice lands you on your own empty wardrobe. Starring an outfit narrows the card deck to favourites only — the badge top-left says which you're seeing — and Browse all (4d) lists every outfit as a shelf row, like the clothes, with a star on each. A bar above the tab bar carries Browse all and New outfit. The tab bar carries Wardrobe / Outfits / You, matching the one on the wardrobe screens in 2a.

2Clickable flow — 1b as home, 1c as Browse all

Tap the tiles, the search field, Browse all, and the + button. Home is the mosaic; Browse all is the shelf layout with every category; the category grid flags pieces you haven't worn. The add flow is camera → pick a category → saved, and lands you in that category. The ··· on an item removes it from the wardrobe, with a confirmation that says what else it affects and an undo bar afterwards; outfits have the same on 4a, where deleting the combination keeps the pieces. Home closes with today's weather and three outfits from your own wardrobe that suit it. The tab bar works across all five wardrobe screens plus the outfit card and its browse list, tapping any item in a category opens its detail — last worn, wear count, the outfits it belongs to, and a Worn today log — and First launch above the phone shows the empty wardrobe a new account lands on. The profile is set at sign-up, so it's no longer in the app header — the Male / Female switch above the phone is just a preview control: same layout, different section names — Male gets T-shirts, Jackets, Trousers; Female gets Tops, Dresses, Bottoms — and Browse all, the category grids, search and the add flow follow it.
